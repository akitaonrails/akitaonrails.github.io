## Conclusion

I'll admit I've still read **very little**. There's more research sitting in the queue, and this counts books only, without the hundreds and hundreds of academic papers, articles, and Wikipedia pages that also went into it.

Even so, I'd say everyone should read **at minimum** something close to what's on this list, counting only the general-knowledge titles, not the technical ones.

My specific interest is understanding how Agile and Lean culture leads to concepts like integrated quality, zero defects, and what you read in The Goal. Especially [self-organization](http://www.akitaonrails.com/en/2009/07/08/off-topic-agilidade-caos-auto-organizacao). From there you get to Open Book Management and The Great Game, which lead to The End of Management and democratic organizations. All of it rests on what's in Linked and books like The Fabric of the Cosmos. In the end, the whole theory closes in a simple, elegant way.
